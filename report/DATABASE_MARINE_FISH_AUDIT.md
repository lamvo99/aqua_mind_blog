### Tang / Surgeonfish (Acanthuridae)
- **Status:** Partial (3 of 80+ species)
- **Current:** Blue Tang (P. hepatus), Yellow Tang (Z. flavescens), Kole Tang (C. strigosus)
- **Missing high-value:** Sailfin Tang (Z. veliferum), Naso Tang (N. lituratus), Purple Tang (Z. xanthurum), Powder Blue Tang (A. nasus), Clown Tang (A. lineatus)
- **Vietnamese terms:** Nẻ nhật (Yellow Tang), nẻ điện (Kole Tang), nẻ bút (Palette Surgeonfish), nẻ sọc (Lined Surgeonfish)
- **Assessment:** Current 3 species provide reasonable entry-level coverage. Advanced hobbyists need Naso, Sailfin, Purple.
- **Action:** P1 — Create 2 entities (Sailfin Tang, Naso Tang)
